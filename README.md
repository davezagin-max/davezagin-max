# Hi, I'm David Zagin

I study Information Systems (minor in Business AI) at the University of Utah's David Eccles School of Business and graduate in December 2026. Across three internships at Enact Mortgage Insurance (commercial analytics, data governance, and enterprise data management) I built AI agents that classify data and map fields between Snowflake and SAS.

I build AI products where **code computes the facts and the model reasons and explains**. I'm looking for new-grad roles in data and AI engineering, applied AI, and full-stack software.
